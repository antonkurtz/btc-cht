# BTC / CHT

**Bitcoin priced in bags of Cheetos®.**

A single-page, tongue-in-cheek currency pair that converts:

- Live Bitcoin price → number of standard 8.5 oz bags of Cheetos®
- Monthly preferred stock yields (STRC & SATA) → bags of Cheetos®

Live at: **[antonkurtz.github.io/btc-cht](https://antonkurtz.github.io/btc-cht/)**

---

## What is 1 CHT?

**1 CHT = the current USD price of one standard 8.5 oz bag of Cheetos®**  
Currently fixed at **$3.97** (common Walmart retail price).

Therefore:

| Asset | Monthly cash yield | Bags of Cheetos® per share / month |
|-------|--------------------|------------------------------------|
| **STRC** (12%) | $1.00 | **0.252 CHT** |
| **SATA** (13%) | ~$1.08 | **0.273 CHT** |

It takes roughly **4 shares of STRC** (or ~3.7 of SATA) to generate enough monthly yield for one full bag.

---

## Features

- Live BTC → CHT conversion (via CoinGecko)
- STRC / SATA yield cards expressed in Cheetos®
- Simple share calculator
- Illustrative CHT price history chart
- Dark / light mode toggle
- Optional Cheetos® dust animation
- Fully client-side, no backend
